## 📊 Featured project

### [Olist delivery audit](https://github.com/nickolis/olist-delivery-audit)

Olist, a Brazilian marketplace, reports that 6.79% of its orders arrive late. Analysing 96,000 orders, I showed that the late rate mostly measures how generous the delivery promise is, not how well orders are delivered. I then built better measures in its place:

- a **route baseline** that judges each order against its own route instead of the promise
- an **early warning** that catches 88% of late orders, where a machine-learning model caught 37%
- a **fair seller scorecard** that rates sellers on the part of the job they control

Presented in a four-page Power BI dashboard, with every step documented in notebooks.

`SQL (DuckDB)` `Python` `Power BI` `DAX` `Statistics` `Data modelling`
