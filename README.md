## 📊 Projects

### [Olist delivery audit](https://github.com/nickolis/olist-delivery-audit)
Olist reports that 6.79% of its orders arrive late. Analysing 96,000 orders, I showed that the late rate mostly measures how generous the delivery promise is, not how well orders are delivered. I then built better measures: a route-based delivery baseline, an early warning that catches 88% of late orders (a machine-learning model caught 37%), and a fair seller scorecard, presented in a four-page Power BI dashboard.

`SQL (DuckDB)` `Python` `Power BI` `DAX` `Statistics`
